# Contributing to Johnny 5

Thank you for your interest. Johnny 5 is maintained by one person,
with help from the models in the room. Contributions are welcome.

## How licensing works here

The core of the project is licensed under the GNU AGPLv3, and
genuinely separable libraries under the Apache License 2.0. The
boundary between them, and the reasoning, is written down in
`LICENSING.md`. Releases through November 2026 were published under
the Apache License 2.0 in full.

## The contributor agreement

Before a contribution to the core can be merged, its author needs a
signed copy of the Contributor Agreement on file with the
maintainer. The agreement is short: you keep your copyright, you
grant the maintainer a license that includes relicensing authority,
and the project commits that your contribution will always remain
available under the AGPLv3. It exists so the project's licensing
stays workable as it grows, and so no contributor's work can quietly
end up locked away.

The text is in `CONTRIBUTOR-AGREEMENT.md`. To sign, send a signed
copy to the maintainer, or say in your pull request that you have
done so. The maintainer keeps a private log of signed agreements
and checks it before merging. One signature covers all of your
contributions.

## Pull requests

- Say what the change does and why, in plain language.
- Keep changes to one subject where you can.
- If your change adds a dependency, say so plainly in the request.
  New dependencies are checked against the dependency license
  inventory before merging.
