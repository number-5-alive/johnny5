# Probe: the seat-of-consciousness circuit, run live

2026-11-04. A labelled probe, not a gate: it certifies nothing
and is published because the failures in it are as instructive
as the success. Pass marks were written before the run.

*Names withheld in this public copy: the test prompt involved a
real client's business. The full record is kept locally; the
redactions change names only, never numbers or outcomes.*

## The prompt

"How far is Ocean Springs from Pass Christian, and what should
[the client] lead with when he talks about [his company]
serving Pass Christian?" Chosen for its natural split: a
verifiable road distance (the maps shelf's job) plus a framing
question whose context only the universal interpreter holds.

## The bids

The universal interpreter bid concretely: the client context,
the drafting itself, and an explicit refusal to assert the
distance, which belongs to the shelf.

The home interpreter (Johnny, butterfly lane) had the right
instinct and claimed the distance leg, but its bid also asserted
"about 11 miles" unverified and named two resources that do not
exist ("community trust logs", the company's "historical
service records"). Finding one: **bids need registry-checking.**
An interpreter can bid with shelves it does not have. The
retrieval discipline in the seat's design exists for exactly
this, and now there is a concrete failure to point it at.

## The translation

A 3-billion-parameter local model, raw, constrained to the
command grammar by schema. Attempt one (7.8 s) produced valid
JSON and the correct split, with wrong semantics: it echoed the
bids as tasks, chose the novelty lane for a lookup, and put
shelves and a mode on the worker that has neither. A repair pass
listing the five errors produced, in 0.8 s, a clean command:

- Johnny / grasshopper / maps shelf: look up the distance.
- The universal interpreter / client context attached: draft
  the talking point from the distance Johnny provides.

Finding two: **at this scale the repair loop is load-bearing.**
Attempt one gets the shape; flagged-error repair gets the
semantics. A future fine-tune would aim to make attempt one
look like attempt two.

## The division

Jev's Choice question type, documented but never before
observed live, returned: **split the work, probability 0.76**,
confidence 0.64, at a cost of $0.0000206. Jev returned the
winning option and its probability rather than an itemized
distribution; the two losing options share at most 0.24 between
them. Finding three: the division instrument is cheap enough to
sit on every dispatch.

## The execution

Exactly the division Jev returned. The maps shelf (a local
routing engine) answered the distance: **27.3 road miles**
(43.9 km), against the 11 miles the unverified bid had guessed.
The framing leg was drafted on the shelf's figure, and the two
halves' work was joined into one answer.

## Score against the pass marks

- Bids name concrete resources: partial (one side yes, one
  mixed; see finding one).
- Translator emits a valid command: pass, at attempt two.
- Jev returns a usable division: pass.
- Execution matches the division: pass.
- The distance comes from the shelf itself: pass.
