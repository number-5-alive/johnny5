# Johnny 5 Contributor Agreement

DRAFT, prepared 2026-11-04 applying SAINT decision record 036s1
(ratified by the maintainer the same day). This draft has not had a
lawyer's review. The maintainer should have it reviewed before
relying on it.

Thank you for contributing to Johnny 5. This agreement keeps the
project's licensing workable as it grows. It is short on purpose.

## 1. You keep your copyright

You own the copyright in your contributions. This agreement does not
transfer ownership to the maintainer.

## 2. What you grant

For each contribution you submit to the project, you grant the
maintainer (Daniel Page, and his successors in the project) a
perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, reproduce,
modify, distribute, and sublicense the contribution, **including the
explicit right to relicense the contribution under other terms**,
among them the project's public licenses and, if the maintainer
later offers them, paid commercial terms.

## 3. The project's commitment to you

Every contribution accepted under this agreement will always remain
available to the public under the project's public license for the
part it belongs to: the GNU AGPLv3 for core code, the Apache License
2.0 for library code. Relicensing under section 2 adds options; it
never removes the public license.

## 4. Your right to contribute

You confirm that each contribution is your own work, or that you
otherwise have the right to submit it under this agreement. If your
employer may hold rights in your work, you confirm your employer has
given you permission or waived those rights for your contributions
here.

## 5. No warranty

Contributions are provided as is, without warranty of any kind.

## 6. Scope

One signature covers all of your contributions, past (if you list
them below) and future, until you withdraw in writing. Withdrawal
does not affect contributions already accepted.

## Signing and tracking

Sign by adding your name to the project's contributor log through a
pull request, or by sending a signed copy of this agreement to the
maintainer. The maintainer keeps a private log of signed agreements
(name, date, contact, and how the signature arrived) and checks it
before merging: **a contribution to core code is not merged until
its author's signed agreement is on file.** This is the process the
project's license position depends on; a missing grant is treated as
a blocker for any future commercial licensing of the affected code.
